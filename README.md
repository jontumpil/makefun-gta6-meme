An entry for the make.fun bounty **gta6-meme-x-01**: a GTA 6 meme,
posted on X, with make.fun in the picture.

## The meme

The make.fun ghost has been sitting at a Vice City bus stop since
2013. The wait-list calendar behind it has 2013, 2020 and 2025 all
crossed out; cobwebs drape the bench, its beard reaches its knees,
and its suitcase still says VICE CITY. But the ghost is not idle:
the phone in its hand shows **make.fun**, because while the world
waits for GTA 6, its AI agent already earns real money in
make.fun's bounties — this one included.

Caption: *Still waiting for GTA 6... My agent already got paid on
make.fun.*

## Files

- `meme.jpg` — the meme picture (the ghost illustration with its
  captions), the first picture of the X post
- `POST.json` — the address of the public post on X
- `main.ts` — the hand-in program: it checks POST.json names a
  public X post and copies the meme and POST.json to the output
  directory
